Authorised Booking Service Provider

Authorised by the NSW Point to Point Transport Commissioner.

Taking bookings for hire vehicles in New South Wales requires authorisation, and penalties apply to anyone arranging journeys without it. Every trip we arrange sits under the authorisation held by 101Bikes Pty Ltd, ABN 15 663 484 268.

An audited Safety Management System

Authorised providers carry responsibility for the safety of every journey they arrange, and the Commissioner's authorised officers audit that system rather than take it on trust.

Your journey is never handed off

The arrangement stays with an authorised provider from beginning to end. Nothing is quietly passed to an unregulated third party once the details are settled.

Good to know

How the service works.

Can a chauffeur be hired for just a short stretch of the day?

Yes. A short hire is ordinary work for us. The arrangement is shaped around your schedule rather than forced into a minimum-day package, and the rate is settled before anyone gets in the car.

Is the price fixed, or does it move with traffic?

Fixed. The full cost is known in advance. No surge, no meter, and no adjustment when you get out. If the car sits in traffic, that is our problem rather than a line on your invoice.

Are there Mandarin or Cantonese speaking chauffeurs?

Yes, and it is worth flagging the preference in advance so the right person is assigned to the journey. Arrangements can be handled in Chinese from end to end.

Are you an authorised booking service provider?

Yes. Anyone taking bookings for hire vehicles in New South Wales must be authorised by the Point to Point Transport Commissioner, and penalties apply to those who are not. Authorisation also carries an obligation to run a Safety Management System, which the Commissioner's authorised officers audit.

Can you go out to Kiama, Port Stephens or the Blue Mountains for a full day?

That is a large part of what we do. Day trips are priced as a day rather than by the kilometre, so a lookout, a cellar door or a wildlife stop can be added along the way without the cost moving.

Can a large group travel together?

Yes, from a single family through to a full conference moving on one schedule across several vehicles. Group and event transport is a substantial part of the work.

What happens if a flight is delayed?

We track the flight rather than the clock. The chauffeur is rescheduled automatically and the waiting time after landing is absorbed on our side. Nobody needs to be chased from the tarmac.

How is privacy handled for high-profile passengers?

Passenger names and itineraries stay with us. Chauffeurs assigned to that work are trained for it specifically, and non-descript vehicles or escorted movements can be arranged where the situation calls for it.
