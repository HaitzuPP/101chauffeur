Before you book

The questions we get asked most.

Can I hire a chauffeur for just a couple of hours?

Yes. Two hours is a normal booking for us. We build the job around your schedule rather than pushing you into a minimum-day package, and the rate is fixed before you confirm.

How far ahead should I book?

Twenty-four to forty-eight hours secures the vehicle you want. For anything sooner, call us. We hold cars back for exactly this reason and we will usually find you one.

Is the price fixed, or does it move with traffic?

Fixed. You see the full cost before we confirm the booking. No surge, no meter, no adjustment when you get out. If we sit in traffic, that is our problem, not your invoice.

Do you have a Mandarin or Cantonese speaking chauffeur?

Yes, and we recommend asking when you book so we can assign the right person. Our office also takes bookings and handles quotes in Chinese, by phone, email or WeChat.

Can you take us out to the Blue Mountains or Hunter Valley for the day?

That is a large part of what we do. Day trips are priced as a full day rather than by the kilometre, so you can add a lookout, a winery or a wildlife stop without the cost moving.

Can you move a large group?

Up to fifty-three in a single coach, and any number above that across multiple vehicles running to one schedule. Conference and event transport is a large part of what we do.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track the flight, not the clock. The chauffeur is rescheduled automatically and the first hour of waiting after you land is included. You do not need to call us from the tarmac.

Can we open a corporate account?

Yes. Consolidated monthly billing, cost-centre tagging, nominated bookers, and a single point of contact who knows your travel patterns. Setup takes a phone call.
